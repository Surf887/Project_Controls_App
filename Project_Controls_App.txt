## THE PROBLEM

Project information in oil & gas, EPC, construction, and megaproject environments arrives in inconsistent formats: Excel cost reports, PDF and scanned reports, SAP/Oracle/EcoSys exports, Primavera P6 files, contractor progress reports, procurement reports, change order and claims logs, invoices, contracts, PowerPoint management packs, emails and attachments, and document control exports (Aconex/SharePoint). Further out: P&IDs, PFDs, isometrics, drawings, line lists, tag registers, 3D/BIM/CAD model exports (IFC, STEP, DWG/DXF, DGN, Navisworks, AVEVA/Hexagon/Bentley/Autodesk), point clouds (E57/LAS/LAZ), and drone/360/site imagery.

The business goal: convert messy project information into structured, validated, editable, traceable, visual, and predictive project performance intelligence.

## CORE PRINCIPLE

The dashboard is not the product. The product is the trust pipeline:

messy source → extraction → classification → mapping → validation → human correction → approval → structured data model → traceability → analytics → prediction → automated insight.

Every number must be traceable back to its source document, sheet, table, row/column, drawing coordinate, model object, schedule activity, or human correction — with confidence score, reviewer, approval status, and audit trail. Assume AI extraction will be imperfect. Design for review and correction, not for perfection.

## LONG-TERM VISION (to be phased, never built at once)

1. Document intelligence — cost, progress, procurement, change order, claims, and management reports
2. Engineering intelligence — P&IDs, PFDs, isometrics, line lists, equipment lists, tag registers, datasheets, redlines
3. Model intelligence — IFC, STEP, DWG/DXF, DGN, Navisworks and vendor model exports, object metadata, quantities, revisions
4. Reality intelligence — point clouds, drone/360/site photos compared against planned progress
5. Project controls intelligence — budget, actuals, commitments, accruals, forecast/EAC, change, claims, cash flow, earned value, schedule, procurement, contractor performance
6. Predictive intelligence — overrun probability, EAC movement, cash flow pressure, schedule-cost misalignment, contractor underperformance, productivity loss, progress overstatement risk, forecast confidence
